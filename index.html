DUNGEONADVENTURE

A pixel-art adventure where every new game creates a brand-new world. Chop trees, mine ore in dark caves, take on quests in the villages – and descend all the way into Hell!

Play in browserFree · no install Download for WindowsComing soon

Works on computers, iPads and phones.

What awaits you?

A whole world, waiting for you to explore it.

🗺️

A random world

Every new adventure generates a fresh map with villages, forests, caves and special places to discover. New here? A short Tutorial teaches you the basics first.

⛏️

Mining and woodcutting

Cave walls hide ores from coal all the way to diamond and mithril. Rarer ores need a stronger pickaxe.

🔨

Craftsmen

Every village has a Craftsman who will make items for free if you bring the materials – and each one knows different recipes.

🔥

Torches and darkness

Caves are pitch black. Place a torch – monsters won’t spawn near it. Nights are more dangerous!

⛺

Bandits and ancient temples

Clear out bandit camps, dodge the spike traps in temples, and break through cracked walls to find hidden treasure.

😈

Hell

Defeat the Earth Golem and a portal to Hell opens: demons, lava serpents, soul collecting – and Satan’s throne at the very end.

🎭

Skins New

Dress up your hero! Use the Skins button in the main menu to choose from 6 free skins, and 6 special skins – knight, mage, skeleton, dragon knight and more – are coming soon. Available in the browser and the desktop app.
